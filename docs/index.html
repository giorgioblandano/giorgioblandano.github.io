Giorgio Blandano

3rd Year PhD Candidate · UniMi

Dipartimento di Informatica “Giovanni Degli Antoni” — Università degli Studi di Milano. Member of PHuSe Lab.

I am a 3rd year PhD candidate at the University of Milan and a member of the PHuSe Lab, advised by Raffaella Lanzarotti and Alessandro D'Amelio.

I am broadly interested in geometric deep learning, equivariant architectures, and vision-language models. My current work focuses on enabling embodied agents to reason over long egocentric experiences, through the integration of spatial grounding, temporal context, and persistent 3D representations.
